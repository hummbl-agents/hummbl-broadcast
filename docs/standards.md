## Rate limits (verified from MiniMax rate-limits page, 2026)

- H3 V2 endpoint: **300 RPM, 30 max inflight tasks**
- We default to **20 inflight** as a safety margin

## Quality bar

- All clips must carry `AI-generated` disclosure overlay (EU AI Act Art. 50, US state laws)
- HUMMBL watermark at `opacity=0.6` minimum
- Resolution floor: 480P (anything lower is not broadcast-grade)
- Clip duration: 5-15s (shorter than 5s is not supported by H3 Max)

## Reliability bar

- Daemon must survive transient API errors (5xx, rate-limit): retry with exponential backoff
- Daemon must survive network partition: stay in last-known state, refuse to publish stale
  buffer past `max_stale_seconds`
- Receipt log must be append-only and survive process crash
- Kill switch must drain in-flight tasks within 60s

## Disclosure (legal)

Every clip must be:
- Watermarked with `Generated by HUMMBL` and `AI-generated video` text
- Logged with prompt hash + model + cost in `receipts.jsonl`
- Publishable as a synthetic media artifact under HUMMBL's AI governance policy
